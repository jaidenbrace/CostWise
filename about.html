About CostWise

CostWise helps consumers quickly estimate what common home and property services should cost before they hire someone. Our tagline says it plainly: Know what it should cost before you pay.

Why this exists

Getting quotes for pressure washing, cleaning, lawn care, and similar work can feel opaque. Homeowners often have little baseline for what is reasonable in their area. CostWise provides simple, transparent price ranges so you can compare contractor quotes with more confidence.

How we think about estimates

Our calculators use typical U.S. residential rate ranges drawn from publicly available market data. They produce low, high, and midpoint figures—not a single “official” price. Actual costs depend on location, property condition, access, materials, labor, and the contractor you choose.

What we are not

CostWise is not a contractor marketplace, booking platform, or substitute for professional quotes. We do not endorse specific companies. Estimates are informational tools for planning and comparison.

The project

CostWise started as a utility-first side project. The site is designed to stay useful first. Future support may include advertising, affiliate or referral links, and other standard website monetization—always secondary to genuine value for visitors.
