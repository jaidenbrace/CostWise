How CostWise works

Three simple steps to a clearer sense of fair pricing.

1

Enter basic project info

Select surfaces, approximate square footage, and property condition. Optional location factors refine the range.

2

Get an estimated range

See a low, high, and midpoint based on typical U.S. residential rates—not a single “magic” number.

3

Compare with real quotes

Use the range as a baseline when talking to contractors so you can spot outliers and negotiate fairly.

Estimates are informational

CostWise helps you understand reasonable pricing before you hire. Actual costs vary by location, property condition, materials, labor rates, accessibility, and the contractor you choose. Our calculators do not replace professional quotes, inspections, or local market research. Always get multiple written estimates for significant work.
