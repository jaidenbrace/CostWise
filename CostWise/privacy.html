Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

CostWise is a static informational website. We aim to collect as little personal data as practical.

What we collect

  • Calculator inputs (square footage, condition, etc.) are processed in your browser only and are not sent to a CostWise server.
  • We do not require accounts or logins.
  • If you email us, we receive the address and message content you send.

Analytics and advertising

In the future we may add privacy-respecting analytics or advertising. If we do, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected and how you can opt out where available.

Cookies

The site may use essential cookies or local storage if needed for basic function. Third-party fonts (e.g. Google Fonts) may receive standard request data when pages load.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not operate a user database for this static site.
