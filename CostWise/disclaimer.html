Prices vary

Actual costs depend on many factors, including but not limited to:

  • Geographic location and local labor markets
  • Property size, height, access, and condition
  • Surface materials and required cleaning methods
  • Stains, mold, oil, rust, or other specialty treatment
  • Contractor experience, insurance, and overhead
  • Season, demand, minimum service fees, and add-on services

Your responsibility

Always obtain multiple written quotes from licensed, insured professionals where appropriate. Verify scope of work, exclusions, and warranties before hiring. CostWise does not recommend or vouch for any specific provider.
